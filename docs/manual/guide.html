Getting Started

1

Read this chapter to learn what Q2 Pod is, how to install it, and how to use its controls.

Q2 Pod brings the iPod classic experience to the Shanling Q2: wheel navigation, Coverflow, a parametric EQ and, optionally, Spotify and Rockbox, all built on top of the stock firmware. With Q2 Pod you can:

  • Browse with an iPod-style Home, menus, Now Playing, Coverflow and four visualizers, in your choice of accent colour, Home layout and battery display
  • Scroll with a wheel that follows your finger, in menus that remember where you were
  • Shape the sound with three simple tone sliders, or up to 30 EQ bands with a live curve and AutoEQ and Equalizer APO presets
  • Stream Spotify (Premium), AirPlay from your iPhone, and Internet Radio
  • Send your listens to Last.fm or ListenBrainz over Wi-Fi
  • Rate songs and albums 0–5 hearts, then browse Top Rated, Loved and rated albums
  • Use favourites, playlists, Most Played, Long Songs, Crossfade, and shuffle by song, album or folder
  • Resume .m4b audiobooks (also split by a CUE sheet) and podcasts where you stopped, with per-folder playback speed
  • Use AirPods stem controls, ear detection, two-way volume sync and smoother AAC audio
  • Look after the battery with an optional 80% charge limit, Low Power mode and a sleep timer

Q2 Pod also fixes the clock resetting after power-off.

Installing Q2 Pod

What you need: a Shanling Q2 with a microSD card, a USB-C cable, and a computer with an internet connection.

Important: Charge the Q2 before you start. Only insert or remove the microSD card while the Q2 is fully shut down (not asleep), or the card can be corrupted.

Install Q2 Pod:

  1. Download Q2.Firmware.*.zip from the latest release at github.com/DiamondBond/q2-pod/releases/latest.
  2. Plug the Q2 into your computer with the USB-C cable. The card shows up as a drive.
  3. Unzip the download and copy update.tar to the root of the card (not inside a folder), then eject and unplug.
  4. Update: on the Q2, open System settings → System Update → TF card update. Leave the card in until the Q2 restarts, and don’t unplug it while it updates.

Tip: Prefer Shanling’s original look? Download Q2.Firmware.*-legacy.zip instead. It has the same features and fixes, without the iPod UI.

Finding your way around

  • Home: a list (Now Playing, Library, Coverflow, Folder, a shortcut, Streaming, Settings) beside the playing track’s cover. The shortcut is Rockbox when it’s on the card, otherwise Favorites; System settings → Display → Shortcut changes it, and Home: Full drops the cover and widens the list. Settings slides in Playback and System in the list’s place, as an iPod’s submenu, with the cover still beside it; Return goes back. Both Home lists wrap immediately at either end. A short Return on Home’s main list opens Now Playing without changing playback; Return there goes back to Home.
  • Folder: opens the SD card directly when no OTG drive is connected. Return at the SD root goes back to Home; inside a folder it goes up one level. With OTG connected when Folder opens, choose SD or OTG first.
  • Lists: four rows per screen with a full-width accent bar; > marks rows that open another list.
  • Status bar: play state, EQ, time, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and battery. The Bluetooth codec (AAC, LDAC…) shows briefly on connect.
  • Page slides: pages slide in from the right and back out on Return.
  • Fast-scroll letter: spinning a long list shows the letter it sorts under (C for The Cure).
  • Artists: open on Albums, with Songs one tap away.
  • Starting up: the Q2 starts on Home. Memory Playback → Location restores your queue, paused; Track restarts the song. In-Vehicle Mode starts playing.

Library and Music

2

Put your music on the microSD card, build the library, and organise it with ratings, playlists and shuffle.

Adding music

Add music to the Q2:

  1. Copy your music to the card over USB-C.
  2. On the Q2, choose Library → Update Local Music.

Update Local Music adds up to 65,000 songs (stock stopped at 20,000), so with a larger library run it again after updating.

Settings, play counts, ratings, resume points and book pages are stored on the Q2 itself, not the card, so they stay put when you swap cards.

What goes on the microSD card

Media folders go at the card’s root, with any capitalisation; each adds its own Library row when present. Caches are safe to delete and are rebuilt as needed (Coverflow and Photos need 16 MB free).

Rating songs and albums

Every song can have 0 to 5 hearts.

  • Rate a song: hold the heart on Now Playing, or choose Hold Play/Pause → Rate. Turn the wheel to choose, press Centre to save, or Return to leave it as it was; tapping a heart saves that many.
  • Five hearts is My Fav: tapping the heart still adds a song to My Fav (five hearts) or takes it out (unrated). A song rated 1 to 4 shows its number on the empty heart.
  • Library → Ratings lists Top Rated (4 and 5 hearts), Loved (5), All Rated and Disliked (1), most hearts first. A row plays the list from that song.
  • Ratings are kept by file path, so they survive Update Local Music but not moving or renaming the file.

Albums have 0 to 5 hearts of their own, separate from their songs’.

  • Rate an album: in Coverflow (also an artist’s), Library → Album or an artist’s albums, hold Play/Pause on the album. Add to Favourites gives it five hearts, Remove from Favourites takes them away, and Rate Album opens the same picker. The row shows the album’s hearts.
  • Library → Ratings → Albums shows the rated albums in Coverflow, most hearts first. Its Sort card cycles Rating, Album, Artist and Year (as Year Order), and is remembered. Return goes back to Ratings.
  • An album is its name with its album artist, or its folder when it has none (disc folders count as one), so a rating survives Update Local Music, but retagging or moving the album starts it unrated.

Playlists and Long Songs

Playlist import, a stock feature, reads M3U files only from the _explaylist_data folder at the card’s root, the folder playlist export creates. Export a playlist once to create it, or make the folder yourself, then put your .m3u files in it and import.

Library → Long Songs (after Hi-Res) lists the songs over 10 minutes, longest first, with their length. The first open measures every song once (“Measuring songs… n/N”); later opens measure only new ones. Cancel keeps what was measured.

Shuffle, repeat and grouping

On Now Playing, hold Play/Pause and choose View Queue to browse the current queue with the wheel. The same menu has Shuffle, Repeat and Group by: Album / Folder. The wheel and touch select an option; the selected option has a check mark. Changes keep the playing track and its position.

ShuffleHow the queue plays
OffGroups in their first appearance order, tracks in group order
AllEvery queued song in random order
SongsGroups in order, songs shuffled within each group
CategoriesGroups shuffled, songs in group order
Songs/CategoriesGroups and their songs shuffled

Play Single Song, Play Category and Play All Categories stop after that song, group or queue. Repeat Song, Repeat Category and Repeat All Categories loop that scope, with a fresh shuffle cycle. Each shuffle cycle visits every song once. Next skips a single-song stop or repeat; category modes stay in the current category. Previous follows what you actually played, and Next after Previous returns to the song you went back from.

  • Album groups tell albums apart as Coverflow does: by album name (ignoring case), then album artist. Songs without an album artist, and untagged songs, use their parent folder. Albums play in disc and track order.
  • Folder groups use the folder a song is in and play in path order.
  • Library → Shuffle → Shuffle Albums / Shuffle Folders loads all scanned songs and shuffles those groups, which can take a while on a large library. Shuffle in an album, artist, genre or folder’s hold menu still shuffles every song it collects.
  • Next Album / Previous Album in the hold menu skips the rest of the group, or returns to the start of the previously played group. Folder grouping shows Next Folder / Previous Folder.
  • Choosing a stock play mode clears these options, except Shuffle during Shuffle Albums or Folders, which keeps the groups. In those modes, Next Album / Previous Album (or folder) lead the hold menu.

Your queue, options and history are restored after a restart; missing files are skipped. Memory Playback → Location restores elapsed time, Track starts at the beginning, and In-Vehicle Mode plays automatically.

Scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz

Save a plain-text file named .scrobble.ini in the microSD card’s root (next to update.tar, not inside a folder). Keep the leading dot and make sure your editor hasn’t added .txt. Copy the example for the service you use, including the section heading and field names, and replace only the values after = with your own. Don’t add quotes.

Last.fm

[LASTFM]
USER=q2listener
PASSWORD=ExamplePassword123
API_KEY=0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef
API_SECRET=fedcba9876543210fedcba9876543210

Use your Last.fm username and password. Get your own API key and shared secret from last.fm/api/account/create (any application name works).

ListenBrainz

[LISTENBRAINZ]
TOKEN=12345678-1234-1234-1234-123456789abc

Copy your user token from listenbrainz.org/settings. The example credentials above won’t authenticate.

To upload to both services, put both completed sections in the same file, separated by a blank line. Otherwise leave out the unused section entirely: a placeholder token still counts as an account, and its failures stop every upload. The file contains plain-text credentials, so keep the card to yourself.

Album artist instead of artist (optional)

If your Artist tags carry guests (“Artist feat. Guest”) and you’d rather scrobble the album’s artist, add this section. Songs without an Album Artist tag still scrobble their Artist; a compilation tagged “Various Artists” scrobbles as that. It applies to listens from then on.

[SCROBBLE]
ALBUM_ARTIST=1

Upload your listens:

  1. Safely eject the card, put it in the Q2 and reopen Library. Upload Scrobbles appears just above Update Local Music when a ListenBrainz token or all four Last.fm fields are present. If it’s missing, check the filename, section headings and required fields.
  2. Connect to Wi-Fi, then select Upload Scrobbles.

A listen is logged when you play a tagged song for at least half its length or four minutes, whichever comes first. Set the Q2’s date and time correctly before listening. The firmware includes the certificates needed for verified uploads, so no .scrobble.pem is needed; a certificate or storage failure keeps pending listens.

Now Playing and Coverflow

3

See what’s playing, rate it, scrub through it and change its speed, or flip through your albums by cover.

The Now Playing screen

Now Playing shows the track’s place in the queue (“25 of 40”), a large rounded cover beside the title, artist and album, the play mode and heart buttons, and a progress bar with the elapsed and remaining time. In Classic the bar is a capsule in the accent colour. Tap the cover to see it full screen. Swipe for lyrics, the track’s details and the visualizer; the dots show which page you’re on.

For synced lyrics, copy the song’s .lrc file into the same folder as the song on the microSD card. Embedded lyrics may not display; use the separate .lrc file.

  • Options: hold Play/Pause for the queue, favourites, playlists, shuffle and repeat. During Shuffle Albums or Folders it also has Next Album / Previous Album.
  • Rate: hold the heart, turn to 0–5 hearts, and press Centre to save.
  • Scrub: double-press Centre, then turn: 5 s per tick. Double-press, Return, touch or wait 3 s to jump. Paused, it stays paused and plays from there.
  • Screen off: a single Centre press turns the screen off.
  • Back where you were: if you got here by holding Return, hold it again to go back.

Visualizer

Swipe Now Playing to its fourth page for Spectrum, Oscilloscope, VU Meters or Halo. Tap to switch; your choice is kept. The visualizer follows what you hear, EQ included; paused, it shows the song and artist.

Playback speed

Changing speed in Now Playing applies to every track in that file’s folder and is remembered after restarting. Other folders play at normal speed unless you set their speed too. Subfolders have separate settings, and the choice follows the file even in a mixed playlist or shuffled library.

Reset a folder to normal speed:

  • Select normal speed in the same control.

Browsing with Coverflow

  • Run Update Local Music first.
  • First open prepares artwork once; Cancel keeps progress. Later opens add only new albums; Refresh Library, the last card, rebuilds everything.
  • Sort, the card before it: press to switch between Album, Artist (then year), Year (newest or oldest first, as Year Order), Recently Added, Most Played and Random. The choice is kept; Random reshuffles each time you pick it.
  • An artist’s albums as Coverflow: System settings → Display → Artist Albums: Coverflow switches Library → Artists → artist → Albums from the default Grid to Coverflow; List shows the album names alone. It contains only that artist’s albums, also when Artists is set to Album Artist. Return goes straight back to the Artists list.
  • Audiobooks stay out of Coverflow: an album whose tracks are all .m4b files or sit under the card’s Audiobooks/ folder. Library Artists and Albums exclude them too; Podcasts keep their current listings.
  • Artwork: embedded art first, then cover.jpg and folder.jpg; otherwise a placeholder. A folder shared by several albums skips its cover.jpg and folder.jpg, since they’d be another album’s art.

EQ

4

Shape the sound with up to 30 bands and a live curve, or load an AutoEQ preset for your headphones.

Simple

Audio settings → EQ opens on Simple; Advanced, the other tab, is the parametric EQ. Tap a tab, or turn the wheel up past the top control (Advanced: past the list’s first row) and press Centre. Switching tabs changes nothing that plays.

Simple is a tone control after Apple’s AirPods EQ: Low, Mid and High sliders, each −6 to +6 dB in 0.5 dB steps.

  • Wheel: turn to pick a slider, the switch or Reset; press Centre on a slider, turn to set it, then Centre or Return. Return otherwise leaves the page.
  • Touch: drag a slider; tap the switch or Reset.
  • Simple On/Off: On plays Simple and turns Advanced off. Changes are heard as you make them while it’s on; edits made while it’s off are kept for later without turning it on. While the page is open, Off keeps Simple’s headroom cut, so comparing isn’t suddenly louder.
  • Reset sets all three to 0 dB.
  • Headroom: boosts get just enough cut to avoid clipping, as Advanced’s Auto preamp.

One EQ plays at a time: Simple, Advanced or neither. Each keeps its own settings, and the status-bar EQ icon shows while either is on.

Advanced: the parametric EQ

Open Audio settings → EQ, then the Advanced tab.

Importing a preset

Import an AutoEQ or Equalizer APO preset:

  1. Copy an AutoEQ / Equalizer APO .txt file to /EQ/ on the card. Channel: L, R and all sections work.
  2. Choose Presets → Import from SD /EQ, then pick the file.
  3. Select the saved preset, then Apply changes (and Advanced: On).

Streaming

5

Play Spotify, AirPlay and Internet Radio over Wi-Fi from the Streaming menu.

Spotify

Streaming → Spotify browses and plays Spotify on the Q2 over Wi-Fi: Liked Songs, your playlists, saved albums and followed artists, Search, the queue and shuffle and repeat. It needs Spotify Premium and isn’t in the firmware: it runs from the microSD card (see “Installing Spotify”, below). The Q2 is also a Spotify Connect speaker, so the phone can still pick it and control it.

Pairing, once: the first time, the page says “Open Spotify on your phone and choose Q2”. With the phone on the same Wi-Fi, pick Q2 in the Spotify app’s device list; the page turns into the menu, and the login is saved on the card. From then on the Q2 needs no phone. Account shows who is signed in and signs out.

Browsing: pick a song to play it in its playlist or album; Now Playing opens, and Return goes back to the list. More… at the end of a list loads the next part. Search asks for the words first, then Tracks, Albums, Artists or Playlists. Hold Play/Pause on a song to add it to the queue. Queue and Playback Settings appear while something plays.

Now Playing shows Loading… while a song starts, including a downloaded song. If no track arrives within 15 seconds, it says “Playback didn’t start”; go back to try again. The phone instructions appear when Spotify confirms that pairing is needed. Long titles, artists and albums scroll so you can read them.

Downloaded Tracks lists the songs you’ve played whose download finished, newest first, and plays them without Wi-Fi. Spotify keeps up to 2 GB of them on the card; signing out deletes them. It needs q2-librespot v1.4. Tidal’s are under Streaming → Tidal Downloads, which plays them as local music.

ControlWhat it does

WheelVolume, as everywhere: the Q2’s volume is Spotify’s

After each power-on, open Streaming → Spotify once and the Q2 shows up in Spotify again; until then nothing Spotify runs. To save battery, Spotify stops itself after 30 seconds paused or stopped once you have left its menu; open the row again to bring it back. An older Spotify add-on still plays from the phone, and the page asks you to update it to browse on the Q2.

Until you play local music again, the playback buttons control Spotify from any page, including local Now Playing. On a Spotify, Tidal or Internet Radio page they always control that service. Picking a song in the Library pauses Spotify and plays the song; casting from the phone again stops local music. Playback carries on with the screen off.

Spotify plays on the headphone jack, Bluetooth or a USB DAC: whichever is in use when playback starts. If you switch output while it plays, pause and play again to move it. Bluetooth and USB need a current .spotify/aplay.sh; an older one always plays on the headphone jack.

Installing Spotify

  1. Download q2-librespot-*.zip from github.com/DiamondBond/q2-librespot/releases/latest.
  2. Plug the Q2 into your computer with the USB-C cable. The card shows up as a drive.
  3. Unzip the file to the card’s root, so the card has .spotify/librespot, .spotify/run and .spotify/aplay.sh. On macOS and Linux the leading dot hides the folder; Cmd+Shift+. shows it in Finder.
  4. Eject and unplug the Q2.

Browsing on the Q2 needs q2-librespot v1.3 or later. To update, unzip a newer release over it; its cache/ keeps the saved login. To remove Spotify, delete the .spotify folder.

Internet Radio

Streaming → Internet Radio plays radio stations over Wi-Fi: your favourites, or stations from the radio-browser.info directory.

  • Search: pull the list down from the top, type part of a station’s name and press OK: the directory’s 100 most-voted matches.
  • Favourites: the stations you’ve saved, plus any playlists in the card’s Radio folder. It starts empty.
  • Featured: six well-known stations to start with, built in.
  • Top Stations: the directory’s 100 most-voted stations.
  • By Country and By Genre: the 60 biggest countries or genres, then each one’s 100 most-voted stations.
  • Now Playing: opens the station that’s playing, or plays the last one again after a restart.

It plays on the headphone jack, Bluetooth or a USB DAC, and carries on with the screen off.

If the stream drops, it reconnects; after five failed tries it shows Can’t play this station. If the output itself can’t be opened, it shows Can’t play: and the reason; please include that text in a bug report.

Adding your own stations

Put .m3u or .pls playlists in the card’s Radio folder: they all appear under Favourites. An .m3u lists each station as a name line, then its address:

#EXTM3U
#EXTINF:-1,Radio Paradise
http://stream.radioparadise.com/mp3-128

Note: There’s no FM radio: the Q2 has no FM tuner.

AirPlay

Streaming → AirPlay opens Network service, where the Q2’s own AirPlay receiver is switched on. Then pick the Q2 from your iPhone’s AirPlay menu. It is AirPlay 1 (audio from one device; Apple Music works), not AirPlay 2 multiroom.

Podcasts, Audiobooks, Photos, Books and Videos

6

Add a folder to the card and the Q2 adds it to the Library: spoken word that resumes, photos, books to read and videos to watch.

Podcasts and audiobooks

Put one folder per show or book in Podcasts/ or Audiobooks/ at the card’s root. They always resume where you stopped and never count as plays. Playback speed is set per folder (see Chapter 3).

M4B audiobooks

Unprotected .m4b files play as they are: copy the original files onto the card, with no renaming or conversion needed. Extensions are case-insensitive. M4B files anywhere on the card resume at any duration and don’t count toward Most Played or scrobbles. Audiobooks/ adds a Library shortcut, but is optional for M4B. Embedded artwork uses the MP4 cover reader, with folder artwork as the fallback. M4B audiobooks stay out of Coverflow and Library Artists and Albums.

Books with a CUE sheet

A book split into chapters by a .cue sheet remembers one place for the whole book, if it’s an .m4b or sits in Podcasts/ or Audiobooks/. Picking a chapter plays that chapter from its start, and it becomes the book’s place once you’re past its first 30 seconds. To continue where you stopped, open the book’s chapters and hold Play/Pause on any of them: Resume, at the top of the menu, plays from the right chapter and position. Music albums with a CUE sheet don’t resume.

Note: DRM-protected files and chapter navigation aren’t supported. As with stock resume, the number of saved positions is limited and the last 30 seconds of a file aren’t resumed.

  • Photos: .jpg, .jpeg and .png (JPEG up to 6 MB, PNG 1 MB); subfolders are albums.
  • Books: .txt and .epub (no DRM); one level of subfolders.
  • Videos: .mp4, .m4v, .mkv, .avi, .mov and .mpg; one level of subfolders.

Video sound plays on the headphone jack, Bluetooth or a USB DAC, and music stops meanwhile. Decoding is done in software, so encodes near the screen’s 375 × 320 play smoothest.

Lock screen

System settings → Display → Lock screen picks what the lock screen shows. Turn it on with the switch at the top, then choose a picture from the grid: Shanling’s own, six built-in wallpapers (Aurora, Sunrise, Graphite, Tide, Ember and plain Black), and your images. For your own, put up to 5 PNG or JPG files (375 × 320 fits the screen) in a lockscreenimage folder at the card’s root.

Clock: Large, just under Time show, sets the time in large type across the top of the lock screen, with the date above it and the battery percentage when Battery shows one; Clock: Standard is stock’s layout.

Bluetooth and AirPods

8

Listen wirelessly in high quality, and control playback from your AirPods.

Bluetooth headphones

  • Codec: the Bluetooth codec (AAC, LDAC…) shows briefly in the status bar on connect.
  • Quality: LDAC HQ is 990 kbps (909 for 44.1 kHz music), LDAC Standard 660 (606), and LDAC Connection (auto) adapts to the connection, including intermediate 492 and 396 kbps rates at 48/96 kHz. Auto uses discrete rates, not 1 kbps increments. Try Auto when HQ breaks up. Rockbox uses the last choice made here.
  • Where things play: Spotify, Internet Radio and video sound play on the headphone jack, Bluetooth or a USB DAC.

Resume on Connect

This setting sits under Bluetooth Headset Volume Control, while Bluetooth is on.

OptionWhat happens when a headset connects
Off (default)Connecting never starts anything (local music, Tidal, Spotify, Internet Radio or Videos); what was playing keeps playing and a paused song stays paused. Play pressed on the headset more than 5 s after it connects works as usual.
OnConnecting carries on as stock, restarting the last song even if it was paused, and AirPods’ own Play as they connect starts the active player.

AirPods controls

AirPods controls work in both the iPod and Legacy builds, with no extra setting needed.

ToDo this
Play or pausePress the stem once. This toggles the active player.
Skip forward or backDouble- or triple-press the stem: tracks for local music, Tidal and Spotify, stations for Internet Radio, and seek steps for video.
Pause by taking a bud outRemove a worn bud. Put the same buds back to resume.

Playback controls reach the active player from any page, including with the screen off.

Ear detection

Removing a worn bud pauses playback, and putting the buds back resumes what ear detection paused. A manual playback command, a different player or track, or a disconnect cancels that automatic resume. The first ear report after connecting doesn’t start playback, and with both buds out playback stays paused. Radio resumes by restarting the current station.

Volume

The Q2’s volume is kept when the headset connects. After that, changing the volume on the headset updates the Q2 and its volume controls, within the configured volume limit; zero mutes. A headset that can’t take the volume gets the Q2’s own software gain instead. AirPlay and DLNA keep their own receiver controls.

Updating, Restoring and Rockbox

9

Keep Q2 Pod up to date, go back to Shanling’s firmware, or run Rockbox alongside Q2 Pod.

Updating Q2 Pod

Repeat the install steps in Chapter 1 with the newest release. Your settings, ratings and play counts are kept. The updater skips a version that’s already installed, so a newer release always goes on.

Custom boot logo

Put boot-logo.jpg in /Q2Pod/boot/ on the microSD card. Use an 8-bit, three-component baseline JPEG (RGB/YCbCr), at most 512 KB (524,288 bytes): upright at 375×320 pixels, or 320×375 pixels with the artwork turned 90° clockwise. PNG, progressive JPEG, grayscale and CMYK are not supported.

Start Q2 Pod with the card inserted, or leave USB storage mode, to synchronize the logo. It appears at the following power-on. Booting directly into Rockbox does not import it. To restore the built-in logo, delete the card file and run Q2 Pod with the card mounted once; the following power-on uses the built-in logo.

Restoring stock firmware

  • Back to Shanling’s firmware: install the official Shanling firmware (en.shanling.com/download/150), such as stock V1.32, the same way as Q2 Pod.
  • If the Q2 won’t start up: copy the recovery-update folder from Shanling’s recovery package to the card, then hold Previous and power on with Centre.

Using Rockbox alongside Q2 Pod

Install Rockbox:

  1. Download rockbox.zip from github.com/DiamondBond/q2-rockbox/releases.
  2. Unzip it onto the card’s root. It adds a .rockbox folder.
  3. Turn the Q2 on. Rockbox starts.

Install the ported themes:

  1. Download the zip from github.com/DiamondBond/q2-rockbox-themes/releases.
  2. Unzip it onto the card. It fills in the .rockbox folder.
  3. In Rockbox, choose Settings → Theme Settings → Browse Theme Files, then pick one and load it.

Switching between Rockbox and Q2 Pod

  • Q2 Pod for one session: hold Play/Pause while turning the Q2 on. The next start is Rockbox again. To always start Q2 Pod, set System settings → Boot to: Q2 Pod.
  • Copy music over USB: turn on with Play/Pause held, plug in, and drag your files over.
  • No card in it? It starts Q2 Pod.
  • Bluetooth headphones with Rockbox: shut Rockbox down (it goes to Q2 Pod), turn on Bluetooth and connect your headphones, then pick Rockbox on the Home menu (above Streaming). Rockbox starts and plays through the headphones, with their volume raised to your maximum volume limit so Rockbox’s volume reaches full; the Q2’s own volume comes back on return. When it shuts down, you’re back in Q2 Pod.

If something goes wrong

  • Rockbox is stuck: hold Play/Pause while turning the Q2 on. It goes to Q2 Pod.
  • Rockbox doesn’t start: the card must be exFAT or FAT32, and the .rockbox folder must be in the card’s root.

Tips and Help

10

Things people often don’t know are already there, and where to go when you need a hand.

Things you might not know

  • Hold Play/Pause on Now Playing for the queue, favourites, playlists, shuffle and repeat.
  • Rate songs with hearts: hold the heart on Now Playing, turn to 0–5 hearts, press Centre to save. Five hearts is My Fav; Library → Ratings lists them.
  • Double-press the Centre button to turn the screen off on any page (videos excepted); on Now Playing a single press does it.
  • Hold Return to jump to Now Playing from anywhere; hold it again there to go back where you were.
  • Wheel too fast or slow? System settings → Display → Wheel Sensitivity.
  • Longer battery: System settings → Power → Low Power and Charge Limit.
  • Guest artists splitting albums? Audio settings → Artists → Album Artist (the default unless you picked Artist).
  • Coverflow order: the Sort card, just before Refresh Library, switches to Artist, Year, Recently Added, Most Played or Random.
  • Screen waking in your pocket? System settings → Power → Wake: Double Press.
  • Want stock’s EQ presets? EQ → Advanced → Presets → Stock presets has Pop, Rock, Jazz and the rest; the ten default bands work as a 10-band graphic EQ.
  • Wheel click gone? Key Tone is the Q2’s own speaker, so it stays quiet while headphones, Bluetooth or a USB DAC are connected.
  • Lock screen look: six built-in wallpapers and a large clock: see Chapter 6.
  • Importing an M3U playlist? It has to be in _explaylist_data/: see Chapter 2.
